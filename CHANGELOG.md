# Changelog
## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-08  "Duel"
- New 3D **Duel** mode (three.js): cover-based paintball duels with hold-to-aim/release-to-fire, 3-pod magazine, swap cover, deflating inflatable bunkers, explosive paint barrels, laser-sight telegraph, kill-cam slow-mo, trick-shot callouts (MASK SHOT, HANDSHOT, BARREL SHOT, QUICK DRAW, UNTOUCHABLE).
- Toon-shaded characters with outlines, 5 themed areas, persistent paint decals, post-FX (vignette, chromatic aberration, grade) with Low-quality fallback.
- New chunky menu over a live 3D showcase: area carousel, Battle / Arena / Gear / Skins tabs, trophies, outfits, share-a-snapshot.
- Top-down team mode kept as **Arena**. Still no ads, no tracking.
## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-08
- Painball: single-player top-down arena paintball for mobile and desktop web.
- 30+ procedurally generated, point-symmetric arenas (portrait and landscape), endless campaign, daily arena + streak.
- Bots with four personalities and tunable skill; best-of-3 matches; 1v1 up to 4v4.
- Persistent paint (your art), share-as-PNG, cosmetic palettes (colour-blind safe), power-ups (shield, rapid, splash).
- Twin-stick touch controls with soft aim assist; keyboard + mouse on desktop.
- PWA/offline, versioned save, accessibility (axe clean, reduced motion). No ads, no tracking.
